# Local card callout voices

These are locally synthesized English callouts, not Steam Liar's Bar recordings or imitations of its actors.

- Male voice: installed macOS Daniel (en-GB).
- Female voice: installed macOS Samantha (en-US).
- Script: one/two/three ace(s), king(s), queen(s).
- Rendered once using macOS `say`, converted to PCM WAV with `afconvert`.
- Playback uses local audio files and makes no online speech requests.
- Reproduction script: `ops/generate-callouts.py`.

Steam confirms that its character voices are AI-generated, but no complete original English callout download was verified during this addition:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/3097560/Liars_Bar/

The current UI edit has not been runtime-tested, in accordance with the user's zero-test instruction.
